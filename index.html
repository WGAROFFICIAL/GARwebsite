Chain of Command

WGAR Leadership

Group Owner

Group Holder

The highest authority and owner of WGAR. Direct contact should be reserved for matters that genuinely require the Group Owner.

1st in Command

Supreme Chancellor

Heads WGAR Leadership and oversees the overall direction, organization, and operation of the group.

2nd in Command

Vice Chancellor

Assists the Supreme Chancellor, oversees leadership operations, and may act on their behalf when required.

3rd in Command

Supreme Commander

Leads the GAR Military Forces and oversees military divisions, readiness, and operations.

Military

Grand Marshal

Assists the Supreme Commander and helps oversee divisions, military leadership, and general operations.

Chancellor's Office

Chief of Staff

Coordinates staff, paperwork, internal communication, and day-to-day work within the Office of the Chancellor.

Development

Technical Director

Manages WGAR development and maintains organization, standards, and workflow across technical projects.

Development

Developers

Build games, systems, bots, maps, UI, assets, and other technical resources used by WGAR.

Military Structure

High Command & Officers

C-3

Marshal Commander

Sector Commander responsible for overseeing and directing an assigned military sector.

C-2

Regimental Commander

Senior commander who may oversee the Officer Corps or major subordinate units.

C-1

Battalion Commander

Divisional senior officer and the first step into WGAR Military High Command.

O-5

General

Highest standard Officer Corps rank and senior military leadership position.

O-3 — O-4

Captain / Major

Field Grade Republic Officers with greater experience and senior responsibility.

O-1 — O-2

Second Lieutenant / Lieutenant

Company Grade Officers responsible for events, discipline, and regular military leadership.

O-X

Officer Cadet

New officer on trial completing the final Officer Academy assignment before commissioning.

Medals & Honors

Retired Officer / High Command

For personnel who honorably served for one month or longer within the Officer Corps or High Command.

Medal of Honor

Recognizes exceptional service, sacrifice, and contributions to WGAR.
