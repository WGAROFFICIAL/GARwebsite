Divisions

Explore WGAR divisions below. Each unit has a different role across combat, intelligence, security, command, reconnaissance, and specialist operations.

Jungle Warfare Infantry

41st Elite Corps

A front-line infantry formation specializing in jungle warfare, hostile terrain, patrols, reconnaissance, and coordinated ground combat. The 41st is built to operate effectively in dense environments where mobility and field awareness are essential.

Specialized Combat Division

Advanced Recon Commandos

WGAR's elite clone combat specialists. ARC troopers are selected for advanced skill, discipline, independence, and battlefield effectiveness, taking on high-risk missions, precision assaults, reconnaissance, and specialist operations.

Infantry

187th Legion

A Republic infantry legion focused on organized ground combat, patrols, defensive operations, and large-scale deployments. Its troops support the wider army wherever dependable front-line infantry is required.

Infantry

212th Attack Battalion

A combat-focused infantry unit used for assaults, defensive actions, patrols, and Republic deployments. The 212th operates as a disciplined ground force during both routine and high-intensity operations.

Infantry

327th Star Corps

A versatile infantry formation supporting Republic ground operations across a range of environments. The 327th provides reliable combat manpower for patrols, deployments, defensive positions, and coordinated attacks.

Infantry

501st Legion

A front-line infantry legion specializing in direct combat, organized assaults, patrols, and major military operations. The 501st serves as a disciplined and dependable fighting force within WGAR.

Clone Force 99

The Bad Batch

A small specialist unit made up of highly capable clone operatives. Clone Force 99 is intended for unconventional missions, difficult objectives, specialist combat, and situations requiring a more independent approach.

Law Division

Coruscant Guard

WGAR's law-enforcement and security division. The Coruscant Guard helps maintain order, enforce regulations, secure important areas, assist with investigations, and protect Republic personnel during official activities.

Jedi Order

Jedi Order

Jedi personnel serve the Republic through leadership, diplomacy, battlefield support, and specialist combat. Within operations they may assist Republic forces while carrying out duties associated with the Jedi Order.

Senate Protection

Senate Guard

A dedicated protective unit responsible for the safety of senators and important Senate locations. Senate Guards provide security, ceremonial protection, escorts, and controlled access during government activities.

Leadership Protection

Red Guard

An elite guarding division assigned to protect WGAR High Command and senior leadership. Red Guards focus on close protection, secure escorts, restricted-area security, and maintaining a protective presence around leadership.

Intelligence

Republic Intelligence

WGAR's intelligence division, responsible for gathering and assessing information that supports Republic operations. Its duties can include reconnaissance, investigations, intelligence support, security information, and operational awareness.

Combat Division

Republic Commandos

A specialized combat division trained for dangerous objectives and small-team operations. Republic Commandos focus on high-risk combat, tactical missions, precision strikes, and objectives requiring advanced coordination.

Chain of Command

WGAR Leadership

Group Owner

Group Holder

The highest authority and owner of WGAR. Direct contact should be reserved for matters that genuinely require the Group Owner.

1st in Command

Supreme Chancellor

Heads WGAR Leadership and oversees the overall direction, organization, and operation of the group.

2nd in Command

Vice Chancellor

Assists the Supreme Chancellor, oversees leadership operations, and may act on their behalf when required.

3rd in Command

Supreme Commander

Leads the GAR Military Forces and oversees military divisions, readiness, and operations.

Military

Grand Marshal

Assists the Supreme Commander and helps oversee divisions, military leadership, and general operations.

Chancellor's Office

Chief of Staff

Coordinates staff, paperwork, internal communication, and day-to-day work within the Office of the Chancellor.

Development

Technical Director

Manages WGAR development and maintains organization, standards, and workflow across technical projects.

Development

Military Structure

High Command & Officers

C-3

Marshal Commander

Sector Commander responsible for overseeing and directing an assigned military sector.

C-2

Regimental Commander

Senior commander who may oversee the Officer Corps or major subordinate units.

C-1

Battalion Commander

Divisional senior officer and the first step into WGAR Military High Command.

O-5

General

Highest standard Officer Corps rank and senior military leadership position.

Captain / Major
