WGAR Code of Military & Community Law

Internal rules, military regulations, disciplinary standards, and review procedures for the Grand Army of the Republic.

SECTION I

Authority & Scope

1.01Jurisdiction

This code applies to WGAR-controlled Discord spaces, Roblox experiences, official events, trainings, ceremonies, deployments, and other activities formally operated in the name of WGAR.

1.02Platform Rules

Roblox Community Standards and Discord Community Guidelines always apply. WGAR rules may be stricter, but may not authorize conduct prohibited by those platforms.

1.03Order of Authority

Platform rules take priority, followed by this Lawbook, published WGAR regulations, lawful leadership directives, and event-specific instructions.

1.04Lawful Orders

Members must follow reasonable orders issued by authorized personnel within their legitimate area of responsibility.

1.05No Loophole Abuse

Attempting to exploit technical wording or unintended gaps in rules to perform clearly prohibited conduct may itself be treated as misconduct.

SECTION II

Member Rights

2.01Fair Treatment

Members should be treated consistently and respectfully during moderation, investigations, training, and military activities.

2.02Notice of Discipline

When practical, members receiving meaningful disciplinary action should be told which rule was violated and what action was taken.

2.03Opportunity to Respond

For serious non-emergency cases, accused members should have a reasonable opportunity to provide context, evidence, or a response.

2.04Appeals

Members may use WGAR's designated appeal process for eligible punishments.

2.05Privacy

Private information obtained through moderation should only be shared with personnel who reasonably require it.

SECTION III

Community Conduct

3.01Respect & Harassment

Harassment, bullying, targeted hostility, credible threats, persistent personal attacks, and deliberate humiliation are prohibited.

3.02Discrimination

Racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic, or other discriminatory abuse is prohibited.

3.03Spam & Disruption

Message flooding, reaction spam, mass pinging, disruptive audio, repeated meaningless content, or intentional channel disruption is prohibited.

3.04Advertising

Advertising unrelated Roblox groups, games, Discord servers, communities, or projects without authorization is prohibited.

3.05Impersonation

Impersonating staff, officers, developers, leadership, ranks, honors, or official authority is prohibited.

3.06Evidence Integrity

Fabricating, altering, or knowingly misrepresenting evidence during moderation or disciplinary proceedings is prohibited.

SECTION IV

Safety & Security

4.01Malicious Content

Phishing, malware, token grabbers, IP loggers, credential theft, malicious files, or deceptive links are strictly prohibited.

4.02Private Information

Doxxing, blackmail, exposing private information, or threatening to publish private information is prohibited.

4.03NSFW & Graphic Content

Sexually explicit, pornographic, excessively graphic, or otherwise platform-prohibited content may not be distributed through WGAR spaces.

4.04Account Security

Members may not attempt to obtain another person's password, token, authentication code, session information, or account access.

SECTION V

Military Code

5.01Insubordination

Knowingly refusing a lawful and reasonable military order during official activity may result in discipline.

5.02Disrespect to Command

Disagreement is permitted. Deliberate disruption, abusive conduct, or behavior intended to undermine official operations is prohibited.

5.03Abandoning Duty

Leaving an assigned critical duty during an active event without permission or valid reason may be treated as dereliction.

5.04Unauthorized Orders

Members may not issue commands, punishments, or directives beyond their rank, appointment, or delegated authority.

5.05Rank Abuse

Using rank to threaten members, obtain improper benefits, bypass rules, interfere with moderation, or retaliate against criticism is prohibited.

5.06Uniform & Formation Standards

During official events, personnel must follow reasonable uniform, formation, and readiness requirements announced by event command.

SECTION VI

Event & In-Game Law

6.01Exploiting & Cheating

Exploits, unauthorized scripts, cheat tools, deliberate glitch abuse, or manipulation providing unfair advantage are prohibited.

6.02Event Disruption

Intentionally disrupting trainings, patrols, raids, ceremonies, tryouts, inspections, deployments, or official roleplay is prohibited.

6.03Unauthorized Killing

Random killing, unauthorized team killing, repeated spawn killing, or killing contrary to event rules is prohibited.

6.04Restricted Areas

Personnel must obey access restrictions for command rooms, staging areas, development spaces, restricted teams, or other designated locations.

SECTION VII

Officer Standards

7.01Professional Conduct

Officers must model the conduct they enforce and avoid abuse of authority.

7.02Event Responsibility

Officers hosting events are responsible for clear instructions, organization, and proportionate enforcement.

7.03Favoritism

Officers may not use their position to give improper disciplinary, promotional, or access advantages to friends or associates.

7.04Retaliation

Retaliating against members for making good-faith reports, appeals, complaints, or criticism is prohibited.

SECTION VIII

High Command & Leadership

8.01Command Responsibility

Senior leaders are responsible for maintaining proper oversight of their assigned areas.

8.02Conflict of Interest

Leaders should step away from disciplinary decisions when a serious personal conflict prevents impartial judgment.

8.03Emergency Authority

Leadership may take temporary protective action during raids, security incidents, exploits, or major disruptions.

SECTION IX

Development Conduct

9.01Project Access

Development files, source code, builds, keys, credentials, and private project resources may only be accessed by authorized personnel.

9.02Asset Misuse

Developers may not intentionally leak, delete, sabotage, or improperly distribute WGAR project assets.

9.03Backdoors & Malicious Code

Adding hidden access, destructive behavior, credential harvesting, malicious remote code, or harmful systems is prohibited.

SECTION X

Investigations

10.01Good-Faith Reports

Members may report suspected misconduct through authorized channels. Knowingly false reports may themselves be disciplined.

10.02Evidence Review

Staff should consider reliability, context, timestamps, completeness, and whether evidence may have been altered.

10.03Case Confidentiality

Investigations may be kept confidential when disclosure could interfere with fact-finding, privacy, or community safety.

SECTION XI

Disciplinary Actions

11.01Available Actions

Discipline may include warnings, timeouts, event removal, demotion, suspension, role removal, ban, or blacklist.

11.02Proportionality

Discipline should consider severity, intent, harm, prior history, cooperation, and repeated behavior.

11.03Immediate Action

Serious security threats, malicious content, doxxing, major exploiting, or severe platform violations may justify immediate action.

11.04Alternate Accounts

Using alternate accounts to evade punishment may extend or escalate the original punishment.

SECTION XII

Internal Justice

12.01Hearings

WGAR may use an internal review or hearing for serious rank, command, blacklist, or leadership cases.

12.02Neutral Review

Where practical, serious cases should be reviewed by personnel who were not directly involved.

12.03Evidence

Reviewers may consider logs, screenshots, witness statements, moderation records, and the accused member's response.

12.04Decision

The final result should state the outcome and any disciplinary action.

SECTION XIII

Appeals

13.01Appeal Grounds

Appeals may argue that evidence was misunderstood, significant evidence was unavailable, the incorrect rule was applied, or punishment was clearly disproportionate.

13.02Appeal Conduct

Appeals must use the designated WGAR appeal process.

13.03Final Review

Leadership may designate a final appeal level for a case.

SECTION XIV

Honors, Ranks & Records

14.01Medal Integrity

Members may not falsely claim medals or honors.

14.02Promotion Integrity

Promotions must occur through authorized WGAR systems or leadership channels.

14.03Record Corrections

Members may request correction of administrative errors in rank, medals, roles, or disciplinary records.

SECTION XV

Amendments

15.01Amendment Authority

WGAR Leadership may amend the Lawbook.

15.02Interpretation

Authorized leadership may clarify genuinely unclear wording while remaining consistent with the purpose of the rule and platform standards.

15.03Version Control

A visible revision date should be displayed whenever the WGAR Lawbook is materially updated.

This is an internal WGAR community lawbook for Roblox/Discord roleplay and moderation. It is not real-world law.
